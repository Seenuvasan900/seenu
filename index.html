About the College

Thiru A. Govindasamy Government Arts College was established in 1969. The college is located at Melpakkam, Tindivanam, Villupuram District, Tamil Nadu.

The college provides higher education opportunities for students from rural and surrounding communities.

The college became a co-educational institution in 1986 and is affiliated with Annamalai University.
